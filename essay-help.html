Essay Help

WHY TRUST ME?

The essays, awards, and salutatorian project mentioned here are available on my Work page.

My goal is to help seniors going through the college process complete their essays with as little stress as possible – as I just went through the same process! I’m headed to UCLA in the fall, but thanks to my essays, I was also accepted to other notable universities such as Notre Dame and UNC Chapel Hill.

I’ve always loved writing, and have won awards in both interscholastic and international competitions. In fact, one of my UC essays was about my love of the English language and how that manifested in various interests throughout my life. However, my favorite piece, and the one I’ve spent the most time on, was the speech that I presented to my graduating class after being named our salutatorian.

We’ll both be on a zoom call and work on essays together — the time is yours, so whether you want to focus on one essay or run through multiple is up to you! I'm also happy to read over essays beforehand so that time isn’t wasted on me reading through them during our call.
